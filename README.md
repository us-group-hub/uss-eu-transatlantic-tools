# USS.EU.COM — US ↔ EU Business Directory, Tax Datasets, Author Hub & Verified Badge Specs

Public datasets, cross-border B2B business directory documentation, author-monetization specs, and embeddable badge guidelines published by **[USS.EU.COM](https://uss.eu.com/)** — the **US–EU Transatlantic Business Bridge** built for both American companies entering Europe and European businesses scaling into the United States.

## Public Platform Capabilities on USS.EU.COM

1. **US–EU B2B Business Directory ([`/all-listings/`](https://uss.eu.com/all-listings/) & [`/add-listing/`](https://uss.eu.com/add-listing/))**
   - Curated searchable company directory across **10 business categories**: *Companies, Startups, Services, Platforms, Tools, Shops, Freelancers, Crypto, Blogs, and Travel*.
   - Self-service business onboarding via [`/add-listing/`](https://uss.eu.com/add-listing/) and dedicated business-owner dashboard at [`/dashboard/`](https://uss.eu.com/dashboard/) (*My Biz*).
   - **Zero-JS Verified Business Badge ([`/verified-badge/`](https://uss.eu.com/verified-badge/)):** Every listed company automatically receives a lightweight, copy-ready HTML trust badge linking directly to its verified directory profile.
2. **Transatlantic Tax & Incorporation Tools**
   - **2026 EU VAT & One-Stop-Shop (OSS) Calculator:** [`https://uss.eu.com/eu-vat-oss-calculator/`](https://uss.eu.com/eu-vat-oss-calculator/) — covers all 27 EU member states, B2B Reverse Charge rules, and Non-Union OSS obligations for US exporters.
   - **2026 US–EU Company Formation Matrix:** [`https://uss.eu.com/eu-company-formation-matrix/`](https://uss.eu.com/eu-company-formation-matrix/) — compares Estonia OÜ, Cyprus Ltd, Ireland Ltd, Poland Sp. z o.o., Netherlands BV, Germany GmbH, and US Delaware structures.
3. **Author Profiles, Subscriptions & Contributor Monetization ([`/u/`](https://uss.eu.com/u/))**
   - E-E-A-T author profiles with 1-click reader follow/subscriptions, real-time notification bell, email digests, and per-author RSS feeds.
   - Frontend article publishing for B2B experts and founders, paired with the transparent [`/u/`](https://uss.eu.com/u/) contributor reward balance & referral program (`+10` reward units per quality guide, `+10` per invited contributor, anti-spam protection, public reward ledger).
