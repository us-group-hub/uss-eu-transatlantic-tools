# USS.EU.COM Business Directory & Contributor Ecosystem

## 1. US ↔ EU Business Directory (`/all-listings/`)
USS.EU.COM operates a structured cross-border B2B directory designed to help American and European businesses discover vetted partners, SaaS vendors, agencies, and service providers.

- **10 Vertical Categories:** `Companies`, `Startups`, `Services`, `Platforms`, `Tools`, `Shops`, `Freelancers`, `Crypto`, `Blogs`, `Travel`.
- **Self-Service Listing & Owner Management:** Companies submit their profile via [`/add-listing/`](https://uss.eu.com/add-listing/) and manage updates anytime from [`/dashboard/`](https://uss.eu.com/dashboard/).
- **Built-In Trust Badge on Every Listing:** Every approved company listing automatically exposes a collapsible **Verified on USS.EU.COM** HTML embed card (zero external scripts, zero cookies) plus a full customizer at [`/verified-badge/`](https://uss.eu.com/verified-badge/).
- **SEO & Quality Governance:** Listings and editorial guides adhere to our [Editorial & Directory Policy](https://uss.eu.com/editorial-policy/) to maintain clean transatlantic B2B signal quality.

## 2. Author Profiles, Subscriptions & Contributor Monetization (`/u/`)
- **Author Profiles & Follow Subscriptions:** Industry specialists and founders get full E-E-A-T author pages with reader subscriptions, notification bell alerts, automated email digests, and per-author RSS feeds.
- **Visual Frontend Article Publishing:** Publish structured US–EU guides directly from the frontend editor without needing admin-panel access.
- **On-Site Contributor Rewards (`/u/`):** Transparent balance dashboard and referral invitation link (`https://uss.eu.com/u/`) awarding `+10` reward units per published quality guide and `+10` per referred contributor, backed by a public ledger and strict anti-spam rules.
